## Prompt

````text
Muse Video Prompt — Ziping Groove in a Qipao (text-to-video, 1 clip × ~8s)
Format lock: Aspect ratio 9:16 (source 1080×1920, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this 9:16 vertical ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.

Master (include with every clip):
DANCE DECLARATION: Dance video — one young woman DANCING alone in a dim Chinese-style interior, performing a slow, elegant in-place groove dance built from a flowing sequence of graceful arm movements. She dances continuously from the first frame to the last — weight shifting foot to foot, hips swaying on the beat — the dancing never stops, not between sections, not at the end. This is a pure dance performance, not acting — no miming the lyrics, no lip-sync, no narrative gestures.
Identity & costume lock: Lin Muxi, a young woman in her early twenties with a delicate oval face, fair skin, soft natural makeup and long dark-brown wavy hair falling past her shoulders. She wears an ivory silk qipao: mandarin collar with frog buttons, short sleeves, a blue-grey floral bird print, a modest side slit, and a small white flower hairpin in her hair. Photorealistic live-action look with natural skin texture. Maintain exact facial features and costume in every frame; nothing about her appearance changes.
Setting & composition: A dark landscape painting panel fills the background, the edge of a dark leather armchair at frame right, deep warm shadows. Locked medium shot: the top of her head sits at 7.5 percent of the frame height, her face spans about 26 percent of the frame height, the bottom edge cuts at mid-thigh, and she stays centered the whole time.
CAMERA — transcribed from the original: camera not moving for the whole clip — one locked eye-level medium shot, no zoom, no pan, no drift; her scale in frame never changes; the painting and armchair never shift.
THE WEIGHT OF THE GROOVE: the dance rides on a real weight groove, not a polite rock. Her weight lands fully from one leg onto the other on every beat and her hips travel about 13 percent of the frame width across each full sway cycle; her long hair swings half a beat behind each landing and the qipao skirt shifts with the weight. Every arm movement is carried by that groove — if the hips read as a small gentle sway, or the arms move while the body stays still, it is wrong.
HER FACE — POISED, FAINTLY TEASING: her bearing is elegant and relaxed — shoulders open, chin at ease, gaze into the lens almost the whole time. The baseline is a gentle closed-lip smile carried by her eyes, upper lids lifted with a clear catchlight; her lips part softly only twice — as the palms push toward the lens, and again at the overhead stretch — each time arriving WITH the movement, not before it. She ends on a brighter but still gentle closed-lip smile, eyes smiling first, mouth never opening wide.
Lighting: a warm golden key from frame right with a soft quality and moderate contrast, tracing a rim along her hair and the silk sheen of the qipao; shadows stay warm brown; the whole image carries a warm amber grade.
Music: a slow elegant pop track at 105.5 BPM, one beat every 0.57 seconds, bars of 4 beats; every accent action lands on a beat.

Clip 1 (source 0:00–0:08):
0-1s | Phrase A (the turn and the push), Bar 1, beats 1–2: She starts in a three-quarter stance, grooving — her left hand resting on her hip, weight rolling side to side; her head turns toward the camera and her gaze locks onto the lens, lips parting slightly then softening, the sway already moving in the first frame. Camera not moving. Avoid: the sway starting late, looking away, a stiff neck.
1-2s | Bar 1, beats 3–4: She rotates to face the camera squarely on the beat, hips still landing side to side, as both palms rise chest-high and push gently toward the lens, fingers spread and extended, each push led by the wrists, stopping short of the frame. Camera not moving. Avoid: both palms merging into one blur, the groove stopping, hands covering her face.
````
