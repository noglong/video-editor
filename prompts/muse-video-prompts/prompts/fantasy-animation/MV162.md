## Prompt

````text
Muse Video Prompt
Format lock: Aspect ratio 16:9 (source 1920x1080, 30 fps; generate at 1920x1080, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this 16:9 landscape ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.
Total runtime: about 28 seconds in 3 clips.

A wuxia ink-magic showcase in photorealistic CG: a lone swordswoman in white performs on the plaza of an ancient stone ruin, and the black ink obeys her — it runs along the ground, bursts from her palms, spins in rings, and finally churns above the whole plaza like a living storm, before draining back into the earth. She never speaks and never smiles; the ink does all the expressing.
Identity — THE INK DANCER: A woman in her mid-twenties with a calm, cold, beautiful face; long black hair bound high with the tail falling to her waist; a flowing white wide-sleeve robe with a long train that drags and sweeps the stone; no visible weapon — her hands and sleeves are the weapons. Her expression stays serene through every explosion.
World & scene: an ancient stone terrace ruin in the mountains at cold dawn — a broad central plaza of cracked stone slabs, steps, stone rails, broken columns, a stone gate, ruined buildings on both sides, layered blue mountains behind; the sky is pale cold blue; the light is clean and morning-cool against the warm gold that lives inside the ink fire.
The ink (a character in itself): liquid black calligraphy ink with weight, gloss and momentum — it flows along the cracks of the stone like a living thing, bursts into splashing ink-flowers in mid-air, spirals as vortices, rises as pillars of black-and-gold ink fire, and at the climax becomes a colossal churning vortex with golden veins above the plaza. It always comes FROM somewhere and GOES somewhere: ground, palm, air, ground. Water is its counter-element: clear rings of water and suspended giant droplets, transparent and bright against the black.
Music and sound: a cinematic Chinese instrumental score — guzheng and strings over deep drums, building with the ink; layered with liquid whooshes, splashes, deep rumbles and the soft hiss of ink fire; no voice, no dialogue.
Camera: bold but smooth — low tracking angles, slow orbits around her, one top-down shot, one flight through the ink smoke; hard cuts on effect beats.

CLIP 1 of 3 — source 0:00-0:09.3. The ink wakes.
Sound (this clip): the score begins low; liquid sounds close and tactile — ink running in stone cracks, water gathering, the first splashes.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE (this clip): escalation by touch — hem, ground, palm, air. Each new element answers her smallest gesture. If the effects arrive before her hands move, it is wrong.
EMOTION ARC (this clip): total calm; she conducts, she does not perform.
[0.0-2.5s] Shot 1 — ground-level tracking, close on the hem: her white train sweeps across the cracked stone slabs as she walks; behind the hem, black ink slides along the stone cracks toward the camera like something alive that has smelled her. Avoid: her full body in this shot; the ink flooding the whole floor.
[2.5-4.5s] Shot 2 — extreme wide, slow rise: she stands small at the center of the ruin plaza, gate and broken columns behind her, mountains beyond; the ink gathers into dark pools at her feet. Avoid: other people; warm sunset light; camera shake.
[4.5-7.0s] Shot 3 — medium close series, gentle orbit: she raises one palm and a clear ring of water spirals up around her hand, droplets orbiting; then both palms push and the black ink bursts beside her into splashing ink-flowers in mid-air; she turns, back three-quarter, and opens one palm to the camera — a black ink vortex spirals tight at her palm. Avoid: her expression changing; the water and ink mixing into grey mud; effects covering her face.
````
