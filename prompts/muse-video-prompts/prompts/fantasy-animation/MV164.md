## Prompt

````text
Muse Video Prompt
Format lock: Aspect ratio 16:9 (source 3840×2160, 30 fps; generate at 1920×1080, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this 16:9 horizontal ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.

Master (include with every clip):
Identity & costume lock: The dragon-tamer — a young woman rendered in photoreal-leaning Chinese-animation CG, long dark teal-green hair streaming in the wind, an ice-crystal crown in her hair, pale skin; when she casts, her eyes blaze ice-blue and frost spills sideways from their corners. Her costume never changes design: a silver-and-ice armored gown — dragon-motif silver pauldrons, bare shoulders, a bodice of translucent ice-blue crystal, flowing teal-and-white silk ribbons and skirt panels that read as a long gown when she is airborne. The white frost dragon — one single colossal serpentine dragon for the whole film: pale silver-blue scales, a thick white mane along its neck, long whiskers, short antlers, four clawed limbs; in wide shots its body spans the entire sky while a human on the ground is under one percent of its length. The palm dragon — one palm-sized translucent ice-blue miniature dragon, appearing only in Clip 1, coiled around the light orb. The demon horde — small dark silhouettes with red eyes on the plain, never shown close, appearing only in Clip 3.
World & scenes: one continuous location — a storm-lashed frozen wasteland under low storm clouds: snow plain, jagged ice spires, distant black ridges, blizzard haze; the upper half of the world is cloud and lightning. Scale rules: exactly one colossal dragon and one woman in every shot they share; on the ground she is a speck against the dragon and the spires — the face-to-face shots are the only exception.
CAMERA — transcribed from the original: hard-cut montage, average shot length about 1.6 seconds; every shot holds one camera behavior only — a locked frame, one fast pull-back, or one slow push-in; no handheld shake, no orbiting, no reframing mid-shot.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE: the film escalates in three waves — summon (eyes and hands call the small dragon first), tame (the colossal dragon's point-blank roar is answered by its bow), annihilate (the strike outgrows the frame: one bolt, then a barrage, then a mushroom cloud, then a forest of light pillars). Effects always land with visible consequences — breath whites out the frame, strikes throw debris and ice shockwaves across the snow. If the dragon ever looks small, cute or weightless, it is wrong; if the final strike looks like fireworks instead of a cataclysm, it is wrong.
EMOTION ARC: awakening resolve in Clip 1 — closed eyes open into frost and she reaches straight at the viewer; unflinching command in Clip 2 — she points into the roaring mouth without stepping back, and her frost-white clawed reach answers the lightning behind her; cold triumph in Clip 3 — she leaves the frame entirely and the land itself delivers her verdict.
Lighting & grade: cold blue-teal grade overall; the key light is diffuse storm daylight from above, broken by effect light — ice-blue glow from her eyes, her hands and the orb, white breath glare, and violet-white lightning that becomes the dominant light source in Clip 3.
Sound: blizzard wind, ice crackling and chiming, deep dragon breath and one roar, thunder cracks, a vast low explosion rumble; all synthesized; no dialogue, no singing, no music.

Clip 1 (source 0:00–0:09.7):
[0.0–4.3s] Shot 1 — Extreme close-up on her eyes, then one fast pull-back to a medium shot at about 3.1s: her closed eyes fill the frame, lashes frosted; the eyes snap open — irises blazing ice-blue, ribbons of frost streaming sideways from their corners; as the camera snaps back she is revealed mid-cast, right arm thrust toward the lens, fingers splayed in a grasp, an ice-blue vortex of snow and crystal shards swirling around her. Avoid: the eyes opening slowly or sleepily, the hand drifting off the lens axis, extra fingers.
````
