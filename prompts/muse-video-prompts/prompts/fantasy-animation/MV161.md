Muse Video Prompt
Format lock: Aspect ratio 16:9 (source 1252x720, 30 fps; generate at 1920x1080, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this 16:9 horizontal ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.

One hand-drawn doodle chibi girl dances her way through a series of photorealistic modern architecture scenes. The comedy is the clash: she is a rough black-ink scribble with flat pale colors; every background is a glossy realistic 3D render. She treats the whole building like her stage.
Identity — the doodle girl: A tiny chibi girl drawn in rough black ink outlines with flat, pale coloring, deliberately crude like a fast marker doodle. Very long straight pale mint-green hair falling past her knees. Two small black antler-like horn ornaments on top of her head. Pointed elf ears. Huge round white eyes with tiny black pupils and thick black outlines — her eyes take up half her face. A tiny small mouth. A white sleeveless dress with a black ribbon bow at the neck and a thin black waist band. Her arms and legs are thin stick limbs, simple black lines. In her right hand she holds a thin black wand whose tip carries a small gear-shaped ornament. Her whole design never changes: same line weight, same colors, same proportions in every shot. She is a 2D doodle composited over 3D worlds, and she never becomes realistic.
World & scenes: (A) a sunlit corridor of tall vertical lattice screens, warm golden backlight, a city skyline beyond; (B) the same corridor at blue-hour dusk, a long glass hallway in one-point perspective with ceiling light strips; (C) a rooftop water terrace at dusk — bamboo planters, a mirror-still reflecting pool, city lights behind; (D) a warm interior with a horizontal light-strip wall and wooden lattice screens; (E) an open-air skybridge at dusk, glass rails, a violet evening sky and a sea of city lights below.
Music and sound: a bright, bouncy instrumental meme dance track at 120.2 BPM, one beat every 0.50 seconds, cheerful and silly; no lyrics, no dialogue.
Camera: the camera is locked or nearly locked in every shot; the doodle girl supplies all the motion. Hard cuts between scenes, no transitions.

CLIP 1 of 2 — source 0:00-0:06.7. Two shots: a sunlit close-up, then a wide corridor dance.
Sound (this clip): the bouncy instrumental track at full energy from the first beat; no dialogue.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE (this clip): her movements are small, stiff and exactly on the beat — stick limbs snapping between poses like a paper cutout being puppeteered. Nothing flows; everything pops. If her dancing becomes smooth, fluid or graceful, it is wrong — the charm is the stiffness. One accent per bar, never two.
EMOTION ARC (this clip): smug self-satisfaction from start to finish — she believes she is the star of this building. Sweet clasped hands, one closed-eye smirk of pure contentment, then huge round eyes staring at the viewer as she raises her wand like a conductor who has never conducted.
[0.0-2.8s] Shot 1 — medium close-up, locked camera, scene A: she stands centered, both stick hands clasped at her chest, the wand's gear tip peeking at the bottom right of frame. Avoid: smooth arm movement; the eyes staying closed; realistic shading on the girl.
[2.8-6.7s] Shot 2 — extreme wide shot, locked camera, scene B. Hard cut: she is now a small figure far down the corridor, center frame, dancing a tiny stiff dance on the beat — arms swinging out and back, small marching steps in place, the gear-tipped wand bobbing with her right hand. Around 5.0s she passes behind a dark pillar and is half-hidden for a beat — a peek — then re-emerges still dancing. She never gets closer. Avoid: the camera following her; the dance becoming big or fluid; her leaving the center of the corridor.
