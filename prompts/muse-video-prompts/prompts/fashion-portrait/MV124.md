## Prompt

````text
Create a 10-second, 9:16 vertical realistic nighttime street portrait video, generated entirely from text. No outfit transformation.

CHARACTER — LOCKED:
One adult East Asian woman, approximately 25 years old, with a softly tapered oval face, dark almond-shaped eyes, defined eyeliner, rosy cheeks and glossy muted-red lips.
Long, voluminous chestnut-brown hair with a loose side part and fine strands blowing across her forehead and cheeks.

She wears a gray high-neck halter top beneath an oversized black knit cardigan, loosely draped below one shoulder, and a short black skirt. A long dark necklace with a pale oval pendant rests over the gray top. Small earrings complete the look.
Preserve her face, hair, outfit, jewelry and natural body proportions throughout.

LOCATION:
An empty paved road at night, with white lane markings and broad crossing stripes. Warm yellow streetlights recede into the distance. The surrounding landscape is mostly dark, with scattered distant lights and a faint blue-lit structure.
Keep the road layout and streetlight positions coherent as the camera moves.

LIGHT AND PHONE TEXTURE:
A small frontal phone light brightly illuminates her face and upper body against the dark road. Warm streetlights add amber highlights to her hair.
Noticeable beauty-filter smoothing, softly processed skin, occasional overexposure during close approaches, low-light color noise and compressed shadows.
Brief focus drift and movement blur occur during quick turns. Preserve the casual social-media phone aesthetic.

CAMERA:
An off-screen operator films with a handheld phone, moving backward or forward a few steps to follow her.
Slight wide-angle perspective, irregular framing and small camera tilts. No gimbal-smooth tracking, cinematic orbit or slow motion.

0.00–1.00s — QUICK CLOSE APPROACH:
Begin with a slightly blurred upper-body portrait as the camera and woman approach each other.
Her face briefly becomes too bright and close, then the operator pulls back and the exposure settles. Wind lifts strands across her forehead.
She looks directly into the lens with a composed expression.

1.00–3.60s — ARMS FOLDED, SMILE DEVELOPS:
Settle into a waist-up portrait. She loosely folds her arms across the black cardigan, keeping her shoulders relaxed.
She gently tilts her head, holds eye contact and gradually forms a small closed-mouth smile.
The camera retreats slightly to reveal more of her outfit and the crossing stripes. Her hair continues blowing toward one side.

3.60–3.77s — TURN INTO THE CUT:
She releases her folded arms and begins turning away. Her hair sweeps across the frame, producing brief movement blur.
HARD CUT at approximately 3.77s, matching the turning movement.

3.77–5.60s — PLAYFUL APPROACH:
In a slightly wider view along the same road, she turns back toward the camera and takes two small steps closer.
She bends forward a little at the waist, looks up into the lens and gives a bright, natural smile.
The operator adjusts distance imperfectly, creating a brief close portrait and soft focus breathing. Her pendant swings slightly with the movement.

5.60–6.40s — TURN AND HAIR SWEEP:
She pivots into a side-back view, briefly lifting one hand toward her windblown hair.
The camera follows with a short handheld pan. Her hair moves across her shoulder, briefly obscuring part of her cheek.
Keep the outfit unchanged and the movement continuous.

6.40–8.20s — RETURN TO FRONT:
She turns back toward the lens, lowers her hand and loosely folds her arms again.
Wind pushes a broad section of hair across her forehead and one side of her face. She keeps looking through the loose strands toward the camera.
Her expression settles from a broad smile to a smaller, confident smile.

8.20–10.00s — FINAL PORTRAIT:
Hold a waist-up frontal view with arms folded and one shoulder relaxed beneath the cardigan.
She makes one small head tilt and a natural blink. Her hair and necklace retain subtle movement.
The camera drifts a little closer, then settles. End on the live image without a freeze-frame or fade.

AUDIO — STRICTLY NO MUSIC:
No background music, instrumental score, rhythmic beat, singing or musical effects.
Only light wind, distant road ambience, soft footsteps and faint clothing rustle.
No dialogue, laughter or lip-sync.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No wardrobe changes, identity drift, changing hair length, missing necklace, extra limbs, malformed hands, floating feet, exaggerated body proportions, pristine studio lighting, perfectly stabilized camera movement, constant sharp focus, additional people, moving traffic near the subject, dissolves, captions, logos or watermarks.
````
