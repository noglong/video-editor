## Prompt

````text
Create a 10-second, 9:16 vertical realistic nighttime street hand-gesture video, generated entirely from text. One continuous shot with no cuts, transitions or outfit changes.

CHARACTER:
One adult East Asian woman, approximately 25 years old, with a small oval face, dark almond-shaped eyes, extended eyeliner, long lashes, pronounced pink blush and glossy rose-pink lips.
Very long, voluminous chestnut-brown hair with loose waves, airy bangs and lifted roots.

She wears a loose oatmeal-gray knit sweater with a large fuzzy brown-and-cream animal graphic on the front. The ribbed neckline folds diagonally across the upper chest, with three brown buttons beside one shoulder.
Colorful beaded bracelets decorate both wrists, with several decorative rings and long pale manicured nails.
Preserve her face, hairstyle, sweater pattern and jewelry throughout.

SETTING:
A paved sidewalk beside a city intersection at night. Gray rectangular paving stones and a narrow reddish paving strip extend behind her.
A zebra crossing, traffic-light poles, occasional passing cars and distant apartment-window lights remain visible against the dark sky.
Keep the background spatially coherent. Vehicles stay on the road behind her.

CAMERA AND LIGHT:
An off-screen operator holds a phone around her eye level, framing her from above the hair to below the waist. Keep enough room for both hands beside her face and above her head.
Small handheld drift, nearly constant subject size and no deliberate zoom.

Soft frontal phone light makes her face bright against the dark street. Warm ambient streetlight catches the brown hair.
Noticeable social-media beauty smoothing, pink cheeks, clear eye reflections, mildly compressed shadows and brief hand-motion blur. Avoid polished studio cinematography.

PERFORMANCE:
A friendly, playful gesture routine with direct eye contact, small head tilts and light shoulder movement. Gestures flow continuously from one position into the next.
Her body stays approximately in place. Hair moves gently with the breeze and her shoulder turns.

TIMELINE:

0.00–1.00s — HAIR AND SMILE:
She lightly brushes the hair over her right shoulder with her right hand, then releases it.
She looks into the lens and develops a warm smile. Her other hand begins rising from waist height.

1.00–2.10s — CROSSED INDEX FINGERS:
Both hands meet in front of her upper chest. She extends the two index fingers and briefly crosses them into a small X.
She glances down at the gesture, then returns her eyes to the lens. Separate the fingers before changing pose.

2.10–3.20s — FISTS BESIDE THE CHEEKS:
She curls her fingers into loose fists and raises them beside her cheeks.
Give two small alternating wrist pulses, then relax. Her shoulders lift slightly and settle as she smiles.

3.20–4.70s — HANDS BENEATH THE CHIN:
She opens both hands and brings them together beneath her chin, fingertips pointing inward and palms angled downward, forming a gentle face-framing shelf.
She tilts her head toward one side and smiles broadly, then returns toward center. Keep the hands below her jaw without covering her mouth.

4.70–5.90s — HEART AND RELEASE:
She lowers her hands slightly and forms one small two-handed heart at upper-chest height.
After a brief hold, she separates her hands and opens them outward. Maintain believable finger transitions rather than instantly replacing the hand shapes.

5.90–7.30s — POINT TO THE WRIST:
She raises her left forearm vertically beside her face with the hand loosely closed.
Her right index finger points toward the colorful bracelets on her left wrist. She briefly looks at the bracelets, then smiles back into the lens.

7.30–8.60s — SMALL HAND FLOURISH:
She releases the pointing gesture and lowers both hands toward chest height.
Turn one palm upward while the other makes a small downward sweep, then switch into relaxed, lightly bent wrists. Keep the movement compact and easy to follow.

8.60–10.00s — OVERHEAD FINISH:
Her left hand rises above her head, palm angled downward like a small roof. Her right hand stays near her waist.
She tilts her head beneath the raised hand, smiles and gently lowers her chin.
End with subtle breathing, moving hair and a relaxed finishing pose. No freeze-frame or fade.

CONTINUITY:
Each hand has five fingers. Track each gesture through opening, contact, release and repositioning.
Keep the bracelets attached to the same wrists and the sweater graphic stable.
Do not repeat an earlier gesture instead of completing the timeline. Preserve natural movement speed without frantic acceleration.

AUDIO — STRICTLY NO MUSIC:
No background music, instrumental score, beat, singing or musical effects.
Only light outdoor wind, faint passing traffic and very soft clothing and bracelet movement.
No dialogue, lip-sync or audible laughter. Smiling remains silent.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No cuts, wardrobe changes, identity drift, changing hair length, shifting sweater graphic, duplicated rings, extra fingers, fused hands, broken wrists, sudden hand teleportation, excessive body swaying, camera orbit, nearby vehicles entering the sidewalk, captions, logos or watermarks.
````
