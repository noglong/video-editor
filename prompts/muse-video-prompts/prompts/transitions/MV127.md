## Prompt

````text
Create a 10-second, 9:16 vertical realistic phone-video makeover transition. Two scenes connected by one concealed hard cut around 3.90 seconds. No reference image is required.

CORE TRANSITION:
An adult woman in casual kitchen clothes approaches a glass sliding door and grips its vertical frame. Use the brief movement blur of the door frame and her forward motion to conceal a hard cut.
After the cut, reveal the same woman in an elaborate evening outfit, standing in the same kitchen at night under saturated red lighting.
No gradual costume morphing, dissolve, flash transition or magical particles.

IDENTITY:
One adult East Asian woman, approximately 26 years old, with an oval face, dark almond-shaped eyes, a small straight nose and full lips. Preserve her facial structure and body proportions across the transformation.

FIXED KITCHEN:
A compact modern kitchen with light-gray stone-patterned walls, a dark-framed double window centered above a sink, a curved metal faucet, pale countertops and dark-brown lower cabinets.
A glass sliding door separates the camera from the kitchen. Its narrow vertical metal frame passes through the foreground during the opening scene.
Preserve the room geometry across the cut. Daylight and styling intentionally change to a nighttime red-lit look.

SCENE 1 — 0.00–3.90s:
DAYTIME DOMESTIC LOOK.

APPEARANCE:
Long straight dark-brown hair, worn loose with a small pale-yellow hair clip near one temple. Minimal makeup.
A cream floral-print long-sleeve pajama set, matching loose trousers, a pale beige apron and light-colored slippers.
She holds a bright-red silicone cooking spatula in her right hand.

CAMERA:
A handheld phone outside the sliding glass door, initially showing her nearly full-body. Bright window backlight, ordinary indoor exposure, slight handheld shake and mild phone compression.

0.00–0.80s:
She stands beside the kitchen counter, facing partly toward the camera. She gives the red spatula one small upward flick, then lowers it toward her side. Keep the spatula in her hand.

0.80–1.80s:
She turns and takes a short step toward screen-right, then notices the camera outside the glass door.
Her head turns back toward the lens. Her expression shifts from distracted to mildly questioning.

1.80–2.70s:
She approaches the glass door, still holding the spatula low in her right hand. The framing naturally changes from full-body to waist-up as she comes closer.
She leans slightly forward and briefly opens her mouth in a surprised expression, without speaking.

2.70–3.90s:
Her free left hand rises and grips the vertical door frame near face height. She moves the sliding panel a short distance while glancing downward.
The metal frame passes close to the lens, with reflections and brief natural movement blur. End on a small forward dip of her head and upper body.

CONCEALED HARD CUT AT APPROXIMATELY 3.90s:
Match her central screen position and downward head angle across the cut. Instantly replace the casual styling and daylight with the evening look and red-lit kitchen. The apron, pajamas, hair clip and spatula do not appear after the cut.

SCENE 2 — 3.90–10.00s:
RED-LIT EVENING REVEAL.

APPEARANCE:
The same woman now has very long, straight chestnut-brown hair with airy, center-separated face-framing bangs and lifted volume at the crown.
Polished makeup: defined eyeliner, long lashes, rosy cheeks, softly contoured features and muted berry-pink lips.

She wears a fitted strapless mauve-brown patterned corset-style top, with a decorative row of small metallic pendants along the upper edge. A dark bronze-brown draped skirt sits at her waist.
Long sparkling chandelier earrings and a dark cord necklace with a crescent-shaped metal pendant complete the outfit.

LIGHT AND TEXTURE:
The window behind her is now dark. Strong red ambient light washes the upper walls, ceiling and countertop edges. A soft neutral frontal phone light keeps her face and outfit clearly visible against the red background.
Use noticeable beauty-filter smoothing, luminous facial highlights, slightly soft hair detail and compressed dark areas. This is a dramatic social-media makeover video with handheld phone realism.

3.90–5.20s:
Begin with her head slightly lowered, matching the preceding movement.
She slowly raises her chin and looks directly into the lens. Her arms remain relaxed beside her body.
Her long hair settles over the front of both shoulders as she establishes a composed expression.

5.20–6.70s:
She makes a small shoulder sway and shifts her weight subtly from one foot to the other.
Her lips form a restrained smile. The dangling earrings move gently and settle.

6.70–8.20s:
She tilts her head slightly, maintaining direct eye contact. Loose face-framing strands move across her cheek with her motion.
The handheld camera drifts a little closer from a waist-up view toward an upper-body portrait.

8.20–10.00s:
She turns her shoulders a few degrees into a three-quarter pose, then angles her face back toward the lens.
Finish with a confident small smile, natural breathing and slight residual hair movement. No additional cut, freeze-frame or fade.

AUDIO — NO MUSIC:
No background music, soundtrack, rhythmic beat, singing or musical transition sting.
Only quiet room ambience, soft slipper footsteps, faint clothing rustle, a light glass-door sliding sound and subtle jewelry movement.
No dialogue, exclamations, laughter or lip-sync. Do not add an artificial transformation whoosh.

CONTINUITY:
Exactly one makeover cut. Keep her identity and kitchen layout consistent.
The door interaction must have believable hand contact and sliding movement.
After the cut, maintain the same evening outfit, necklace, earrings, hair and red lighting until the end.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No music of any kind, extra cuts, slow costume morphing, flashing transition, face replacement, extra hands, warped fingers, duplicated spatula, residual apron after the reveal, changing kitchen geometry, floating jewelry, exaggerated dancing, plastic facial anatomy, subtitles, logos or watermarks.
````
