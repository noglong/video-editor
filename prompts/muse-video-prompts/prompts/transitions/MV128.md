- English title / 英文标题: Stairwell, two shots and one hard cut

## Prompt

````text
Create a 10-second, 16:9 horizontal video with exactly TWO shots and one hard cut at 1.67 seconds. Begin with ordinary phone footage in a stairwell, then cut to a dreamy cinematic woodland balcony. No reference image is required.

SHOT 1 — 0.00–1.67s:
REALISTIC INDOOR STAIRWELL.

A narrow apartment stairwell with plain white walls, dark-gray steps, a metal handrail along screen-right and a red vertical utility pipe beside the landing. A bright rectangular window at the top slightly overexposes the exterior.

An adult East Asian man in his twenties stands on the upper landing. He has short black hair and wears a loose white T-shirt with a small red graphic on the back, black athletic shorts with large white angular trim, and casual footwear.

CAMERA:
Handheld phone camera positioned several steps below him, looking steeply upward. Wide-angle perspective, slightly tilted framing and small walking-induced shake. Cool bluish indoor light, muted colors and ordinary compressed phone detail.

ACTION:
0.00–0.70s:
He stands partly side-on near the window, glancing down toward the stairs. The camera rises slightly as the operator advances a short distance.

0.70–1.67s:
He turns his shoulders away from the camera and settles facing the window, showing his back. Keep the motion casual and brief. End with his upright figure centered against the bright opening.

HARD CUT AT 1.67s.
No dissolve, flash, portal or visible transformation.

SHOT 2 — 1.67–10.00s:
DREAMLIKE WOODLAND BALCONY.

COMPOSITION:
Continue a steep upward-looking composition, now from beneath dense tree branches toward the upper balcony of a rustic two-story timber house.

The house stretches diagonally across the frame because of the low viewing angle and a restrained camera roll. Dark wooden posts, weathered timber walls, projecting roof beams and a narrow metal balcony railing remain structurally consistent.

Large dark-green leaves hang close to the lens across the upper and side edges. More branches overlap the balcony, creating strong foreground depth.

Warm amber light glows through tall wooden-framed windows and doors behind the railing. Small warm bulbs trace parts of the eaves. Bright pale-cyan daylight filters through the canopy above, contrasting with the golden interior.

CHARACTER:
One adult man stands on the balcony with his back toward the camera, near the center of the composition. He wears a white long-sleeve shirt, a dark fitted vest and dark trousers. His figure is small within the architectural scene. Preserve his clothing and proportions throughout this shot.

ACTION AND CAMERA:
1.67–3.20s:
Establish the balcony immediately after the cut. The man stands facing the warmly illuminated doorway, one arm resting lightly near the railing.
The camera begins a very slow lateral drift beneath the branches. Nearby leaves move across the frame faster than the distant house, producing natural parallax.

3.20–5.80s:
He makes a small weight shift and subtly inclines his head toward the doorway. Avoid a large turn or walk.
Continue the slow camera drift and slight upward tilt. Wind gently moves the foreground leaves, intermittently revealing the railing and warm windows.

5.80–8.20s:
The camera gradually reveals more of the projecting roof and balcony corner while maintaining the same low angle. The man remains near his original position, largely seen from behind.
A few softly blurred leaves pass across portions of his silhouette without completely hiding the scene.

8.20–10.00s:
Ease the camera movement almost to a stop. Hold the contrast between dark foliage, glowing amber windows and bright sky.
The man remains quietly upright. Leaves continue to sway and warm lights remain steady. End on the live scene, with no fade or freeze-frame.

VISUAL CONTRAST:
Shot 1 is mundane, cool, lightly shaky and phone-recorded.
Shot 2 is richly atmospheric and softly cinematic: deep green shadows, warm amber light, luminous sky, gentle optical bloom, softened detail and subtle motion softness in nearby foliage.

The exterior should feel like a photographed dreamlike woodland retreat, with believable timber, glass, railings and layered vegetation. Keep the architecture stable throughout the moving view.

PROPOSED AUDIO:
Brief indoor footsteps and quiet stairwell ambience before the cut. At 1.67s, introduce a soft musical accent and an atmospheric instrumental with sustained warm tones. Light wind and rustling leaves accompany the woodland shot. No dialogue or lip-sync.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No additional cuts, close-up portraits, visible costume morphing, portals, rapid camera orbit, sudden zooms, walking off the balcony, duplicated people, changing railings, warped timber beams, floating architecture, flickering windows, excessive glowing particles, captions, logos or watermarks.
````
