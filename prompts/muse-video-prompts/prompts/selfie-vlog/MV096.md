- English title / 英文标题: Vesper vlog: autumn café in Toronto

## Prompt

````text
Video concept: A realistic 20-second lifestyle vlog filmed by Vesper herself. Use Vesper's provided character reference for her identity and body proportions. Never alter her identity.

Location: A beautiful autumn Saturday morning in a cozy Toronto neighborhood. Tree-lined streets with golden leaves, independent cafés, warm brick storefronts, and a relaxed weekend atmosphere.

Outfit: An oversized oatmeal-colored knit sweater, dark brown pleated mini skirt, black sheer tights, brown leather ankle boots, a small chocolate-brown crossbody bag, and delicate gold earrings.

Hairstyle and makeup: Loose, naturally styled hair with soft waves, understated fresh makeup, warm pink lips.

0–4 seconds | Selfie introduction
Vesper walks down the sidewalk holding her phone at arm's length, filming herself with the front-facing camera. The image has slight natural walking movement.

She smiles at the screen and casually says in Mandarin:

“早上好！今天出来找家好喝的咖啡。”

A little autumn wind blows loose strands of her hair across her face. She casually brushes them away.

4–8 seconds | Discovering a café

A natural vlog jump cut switches to her phone's rear camera. She films a charming café exterior, the wooden entrance door, handwritten coffee menu, and pastries behind the window.
She pushes the door open. A small bell rings.

She says from behind the camera:

“就这家吧，看着还不错。”

8–13 seconds | Coffee moment

Several quick, realistic phone-recorded clips: a barista pours steamed milk into a ceramic cup, a close-up of a freshly baked croissant, and Vesper's hand placing her coffee on a small wooden table beside the window.

The camera switches back to a front-facing selfie shot. Vesper takes a small sip, pauses for a moment, raises her eyebrows pleasantly, and smiles.
She says:

“嗯！这个真的可以。”

13–17 seconds | A funny little accident

Vesper picks up her croissant while filming herself. A few flaky crumbs accidentally fall onto her sweater.

She glances downward, looks back into the camera, laughs naturally, and brushes the crumbs away.

Her reaction is spontaneous and slightly embarrassed, not exaggerated.
17–20 seconds | Ending the vlog

A quick cut to Vesper walking outside with her takeaway coffee. She films herself against the beautiful golden autumn trees.

She gives the camera a bright, relaxed smile and says:

“好啦，继续逛逛！”

She turns the phone outward to reveal the autumn street as the clip ends naturally.
Filming style: Authentic smartphone vlog, casual front-facing selfie footage mixed with rear-camera POV clips, natural handheld shake, imperfect but believable framing, realistic phone autofocus and exposure adjustments, natural jump cuts, genuine everyday spontaneity.

Performance: Friendly, lively, charming, slightly playful. Vesper behaves like a real person enjoying her weekend, never like a model performing for a commercial. Natural eye contact with the phone screen, conversational expressions, realistic pauses and gestures.

Lighting: Real autumn morning daylight, slightly warm sunlight outdoors, soft natural window light inside the café. Consistent lighting appropriate to each location.

Audio: Clear natural Mandarin speech, accurate lip synchronization, realistic café ambience, espresso machine sounds, doorbell, quiet conversations, footsteps, outdoor traffic, autumn wind, and natural laughter. No artificial narrator.

Realism and continuity: Consistent Vesper identity, clothing, hairstyle, accessories, coffee cup, and physical environment. Correct finger anatomy, realistic object handling, believable walking movement, natural interaction with the camera and surrounding objects.

Avoid: Fashion commercial aesthetics, dramatic cinematic camera moves, excessive beauty filters, unnatural posing, artificial facial expressions, robotic gestures, exaggerated acting, identity drift, distorted hands, unrealistic background movement, floating objects, subtitles, logos, and watermarks.
````
