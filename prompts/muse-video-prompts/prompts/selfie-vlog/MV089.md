## Prompt

````text
A 10-second live-action video with authentic smartphone selfie footage, filmed vertically in 9:16. Play entirely in real time at normal 1× speed, without speeding up or slowing down. One continuous take, maintaining a close-range facial selfie throughout, with no cuts.

She sits in the back seat of a moving taxi late at night, resting comfortably against the seat. She holds the phone herself in one hand and films with the front-facing camera. The phone captures her face from a slightly offset low angle, approximately 30–40 degrees upward, rather than vertically beneath her chin. The camera is very close, with her face filling most of the frame and the dress’s shoulder straps occasionally visible along the bottom edge. The phone and the hand holding it remain outside the frame throughout. Maintain a natural selfie perspective without zooming.

She appears to have had a small amount to drink and is mildly tipsy but fully aware. Her cheeks are slightly flushed, her eyes relaxed, her blinking a little slower, and her smile natural. She looks relaxed, happy, and slightly shy, without appearing heavily intoxicated, unconscious, or physically out of control.

Red, blue, and purple city neon lights sweep across her face through the taxi windows, creating natural changes in brightness and alternating warm and cool tones. The nighttime scenery outside continues moving backward. Preserve gentle vehicle bumps, natural handheld shake, background motion blur, smartphone low-light noise, and mobile-video compression artifacts. Her face remains clearly recognizable, illuminated by passing lights outside the windows.

0–2 seconds:
The video begins already framed on her face. She rests comfortably against the rear seat, checks the framing on the phone screen, and gently adjusts the phone’s angle. Her cheeks are slightly pink, a faint smile rests on her lips, and loose strands fall naturally across her forehead. Warm yellow streetlight slowly sweeps across her face.

2–5 seconds:
She shifts her gaze from the screen to the lens, looks quietly into it for a moment, and gradually forms a natural, closed-mouth smile. Her head tilts gently to one side, and her eyes narrow slightly with her smile, as though she is enjoying how she looks in the selfie. She does not speak or make any speaking mouth movements.

5–7 seconds:
She briefly glances toward the moving lights outside the window, turning her head only slightly while keeping the phone aimed at her face. She then naturally looks back into the lens, blinks softly, and retains a small smile. Blue-violet neon light passes along the side of her face.

7–10 seconds:
After looking back into the lens, her smile grows slightly, conveying relaxed, mildly tipsy happiness with a touch of shyness. Her eyes soften into a smiling expression. She gently tilts her head, then slowly straightens it, as though silently interacting with the camera. The fine strands around her forehead sway slightly with her movement, while her tied-back hairstyle remains unchanged.

End on her smiling, moving expression as she looks into the lens. Gentle vehicle movement continues. No freeze-frame, pull-back, or cut.

Audio:
Only the sound of the moving taxi, subtle cabin noise, and distant city traffic. No dialogue, narration, humming, audible laughter, or background music throughout. Express her smile through her eyes, the corners of her mouth, and small head movements.

Keep the reference character’s identity stable. Her clothing, hairstyle, hair color, accessories, and facial proportions remain unchanged. Expressions change naturally without altering her facial identity. Only she appears in the frame. Do not add bottles, drinking glasses, or other props.

Restrictions:
No text, subtitles, logos, or watermarks. No speaking mouth movements, dialogue, male voices, narration, or additional people. No exaggerated drunken behavior, eye-rolling, loss of consciousness, or crying. No tongue gestures, lip-biting, kissing the camera, or additional gestures. No wardrobe changes, loose flowing hair, curly-hair transformation, or replacement accessories. No slow motion, sped-up playback, cuts, gimbal-like stabilization, cinematic lighting, excessive beauty filtering, plastic-looking skin, facial drift, or changes in character identity.
````
