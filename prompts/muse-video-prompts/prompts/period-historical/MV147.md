## Prompt

````text
10-second single continuous take, one unbroken flowing shot, 16:9, hyper-photorealistic live-action Chinese period film, 35mm cinema lens, real shallow depth of field, low-saturation Song painting palette, light film grain, soft warm morning side-light, natural water mist and air perspective, 1080p visual quality. Midsummer early morning in a Jiangnan water town countryside, Southern Song dynasty: the lotus pond meets a grassy shore; narrow field paths wind between lush green rice paddies; a small village of white-gray walls and gray-tiled wooden houses in thin mist; NO high mountains.

SAME WOMAN: early twenties, natural clear elegant East Asian face, real skin texture, NO modern makeup; long black hair in a low Song-style bun fixed with a small white jade hairpin, a few loose strands at her temples; light lotus-pink cross-collar narrow-sleeve top, moon-white beizi, pale cyan long skirt, real silk-linen texture; sleeves slightly rolled up.

EXPRESSION RULE - VERY IMPORTANT: her face is CALM and serene the entire time, with at most a faint gentle smile. NO big grins, NO constant smiling, NO silly goofy smile, NO laughing out loud. Quiet, composed, graceful.

DRAGONFLY RULE - VERY IMPORTANT: a small real red dragonfly FOLLOWS HER the whole way - it flies behind her as she walks, darting playfully around her in the background, never leaving her side, all the way from the shore to inside the courtyard.

DOOR RULE - VERY IMPORTANT: at the end she pushes the wooden gate open and WALKS INSIDE the courtyard. She does NOT stop at the doorway - she keeps walking in.

One continuous story. 0.00-2.50s: the narrow dark-wood boat glides to the grassy shore; she rises gracefully, holding a fresh pink-white LOTUS FLOWER in one hand and the round bamboo basket of lotus flowers and pods in the other, and steps lightly ashore; the small red dragonfly is already fluttering behind her. 2.50-6.50s: the camera follows her in a smooth gentle tracking shot as she walks home along a narrow field ridge between green rice paddies, the lotus flower held loosely at her side; the red dragonfly flies behind her, darting playfully around her shoulders and the flower in the background; morning light filters through, a breeze moves her beizi hem; her steps are light, unhurried, at ease. 6.50-10.00s: she reaches her home - a small Jiangnan courtyard house with whitewashed walls, gray roof tiles and a weathered wooden gate; she pushes the gate open with one hand and WALKS STRAIGHT INSIDE into the sunlit courtyard with its stone well and potted plants, NOT stopping at the door; the red dragonfly follows her in, circling above the courtyard; she turns with a calm tender faint smile as it loops around her; a foreground branch sways gently in the breeze to end.

The dragonfly is realistically sized, flies like a real insect, is NEVER caught or harmed, and NEVER vanishes unnaturally.

Camera: one continuous stable take - low tracking from the boat, smooth side-follow along the field path, following her through the gate into the courtyard; no fast moves, no sudden zoom, no shake, no jelly wobble.

Sound: boat touching shore, soft footsteps on grass and the field ridge, rustling rice leaves, the wooden gate creaking open, distant birdsong, summer cicadas, light dragonfly wing flutters; extremely light xiao flute and guqin, never covering the ambience; NO dialogue.

Entire film: NO subtitles, NO Chinese characters, NO signs, NO logos, NO watermarks. NO modern buildings, NO wires, NO plastic, NO modern farm machinery. NO Tang/Ming/Qing costumes, NO loose modern hair, NO influencer makeup, NO xianxia ribbons. NO face changes, NO costume changes, NO extra fingers, NO fused palms, NO limb clipping. NO oversized or cartoon dragonfly. NO over-smoothed skin, NO plastic CG look, NO anime feel, NO screen-filling petals, NO whip pans, NO aimless rotation.
````
