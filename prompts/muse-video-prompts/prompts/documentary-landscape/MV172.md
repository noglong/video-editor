## Prompt

````text
This was made by Muse. The effect is stunning.

🤖 And yes — it is AI-generated, not real footage.

🌅 Dawn in the Sahel. A herder walks his zebu cattle forward, dust settling behind them.
🐂 One walk. 🎥 One camera move. 💡 One dawn light.

💡 That is the whole prompt trick:
Do not ask for five actions. Ask for one action, and let it finish.
Wide shot, simple walking, dust falling back to the ground — that is what survives generation.

🎯 If you generate video, start here:
One complete action + one camera move + one light source + what must NOT move.

📋 Full prompt:
Humanistic documentary footage, 10 seconds, 16:9, 24fps, 1080p, Sahel at dawn, like a real documentary crew happened to be there. A Fulani herder walks behind a small group of zebu cattle on a dusty path, driving them forward at a slow, steady walk. Camera sits low at eye level beside dry grass, a blurred tuft of dry grass crosses the left foreground. Midground, off-center: the herder and the cattle, walking away from the camera in the same direction, never looking at the lens. Background: flat savanna, a lone acacia tree and a low golden sunrise, softly hazy, one light source only, warm dawn light from ahead, shadows stay naturally long and dark. Timecodes: 0-3 seconds, the herd starts walking, dust lifts behind their feet; 3-7 seconds, one complete action only — the herder and cattle keep walking forward at the same slow pace, dust rises and then settles behind them in one wind direction, hooves truly contact the dusty ground, no clipping, no floating; 7-10 seconds, aftermath — the herd keeps walking into the distance, dust settles behind them, the frame holds still. Camera: one slow push-in only, under 0.5 meters per second, no pan, no zoom change, no cut. Wide to medium shot, imperfect framing, horizon slightly offset, natural skin and cloth texture, worn robes, no staged perfection, no glowing halos.

🔖 Save this one if you generate video.
````
