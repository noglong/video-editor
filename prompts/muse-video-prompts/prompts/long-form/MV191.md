## Prompt

````text
Muse Video Prompt
Format lock: Aspect ratio 16:9 (source 3840x2160, 30 fps; generate at 1920x1080, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this 16:9 landscape ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.
Total runtime: about 58 seconds in 6 clips.

A giant-object healing fantasy in photorealistic CG, shot like a summer daydream: a young woman in pale hanfu drifts through a world where leaves, peaches, grapes and lemons are the size of hills, and she is small among them. Everything moves at the speed of a nap. It is quietly a product film, but the wonder does the selling — the little colored bottles appear only near the end, as if the garden had grown them too.
Identity — THE GARDENER: A young woman with a clear, fresh, unadorned beauty; long black hair half-bound with a small flower pin and a pale-blue ribbon; a pale green-to-white gradient wide-sleeve hanfu that floats when she moves. Her gestures are few and small — touching, holding up, gazing, tasting — and her expression is one long held breath of contentment.
World & scenes: (A) a lotus pond under giant lotus leaves, a hammock of white gauze strung between the leaves over the water; (B) a lake of giant peaches, mist on the water, a small wooden boat; (C) a vineyard of giant grape clusters hanging like chandeliers; (D) a grove of giant lemons, several halved, glowing against the sun; (E) a turquoise stream valley under giant leaves, glass orbs resting on the water; (F) a carved stone table under the leaves bearing small colorful drink bottles — pink, purple, yellow — arranged with fresh fruit and ice.
Scale law (never broken): she stands between one-third and one-fifth the height of the giant fruit around her; every touch has a small physical answer — a fingertip sinks a fraction into peach skin, a grape cluster sways almost imperceptibly, a leaf dips under her palm.
Music and sound: a soft healing instrumental — piano, light strings, air; water, leaves, and distant summer insects underneath; no voice, no dialogue.
Camera: slow floating moves throughout — gentle rises, drifts and arcs; hard cuts between gardens; nothing ever hurries.

CLIP 1 of 6 — source 0:00-0:09.7. Scene A: the lotus hammock.
Sound (this clip): water and leaves; the score at its softest.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE (this clip): sleep, then the smallest possible waking — a leaf held, eyes opening. If she sits up quickly, it is wrong.
EMOTION ARC (this clip): she belongs to the afternoon completely; waking is a favor she does it slowly.
[0.0-4.0s] Shot 1 — framed through leaves into close-up, slow push: the white-gauze hammock hangs between giant lotus leaves over the pond; inside it she sleeps on the gauze, face in close-up, utterly peaceful; her raised hand holds one tiny mint leaf between two fingers, the only normal-sized green in the frame. Avoid: her waking in this shot; the hammock swinging wide; other people.
[4.0-9.7s] Shot 2 — wide, slow rise and drift: the hammock-boat drifts among the giant leaves; she stirs, sits up slowly among the white fabric, looking around at the green immensity with sleepy wonder, one hand trailing near the water. Avoid: standing; rowing hard; the leaves shrinking to normal size.
Whole-clip Avoid: no on-screen text or calligraphy overlays, no watermark, no product bottles yet, no modern objects.

CLIP 2 of 6 — source 0:09.7-0:19.4. Scene B: the peach lake.
Sound (this clip): water against the boat; a slightly warmer string line.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE (this clip): drifting between soft mountains — the boat's pace is the lake's pace, and the touch on the peach must dent it, barely.
EMOTION ARC (this clip): quiet awe; she reaches for the fruit the way you reach for a cheek.
[9.7-14.5s] Shot 3 — wide, slow lateral drift: the small boat carries her across the misty lake between giant peaches, their pink skins catching the sun, reflections doubling them in the water. Avoid: speed; waves; peaches at normal size.
````
