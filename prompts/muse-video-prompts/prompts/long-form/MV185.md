- English title / 英文标题: Qipao mahjong in 30 seconds, 8 shots

## Prompt

````text
《旗袍麻将》30秒短片 · 提示词

成片规格：30 秒 / 16:9 横屏 / 8 个分镜 / 暖调复古胶片风 / BGM《相思遥》

1. 一句话需求（给 AI 的总指令）
生成一条 30 秒、16:9 横屏短片：4 位 20 岁出头的中国年轻女子，
穿旗袍（粉/黑金/米白/红）在昏暗复古中式房间里打麻将。
暖黄灯光、35mm 胶片质感、浅景深、氛围慵懒亲密。
8 个镜头约 3.75 秒/个：粉旗袍胸口特写 → 摆牌手部特写 →
粉旗袍手臂中近景 → 粉衣女子垂眸浅笑特写 → 四人全景 →
双人中景 → 门后朦胧远景 → 三人欢笑收尾。

2. 风格母版（每个分镜提示词都要带上这一段，原样复用）
Style master（英文，逐字复用）：
Cinematic 35mm film look, warm vintage Chinese aesthetic.
Dimly lit vintage Chinese room, dark carved wood, lattice windows,
glowing warm floor lamp. Warm amber tungsten lighting, soft haze,
gentle rim light on hair. Shallow depth of field, soft film grain,
nostalgic warm color grade. Elegant, languid, intimate mood.

3. 人物设定（锁定描述词，四个分镜间逐字复用，保证人物一致）
代号
锁定描述词

A「粉」
young East Asian woman, early 20s, shoulder-length wavy black hair, pink silk embroidered qipao with floral embroidery and pearl brooch
B「黑金」
young East Asian woman, early 20s, short black bob, black sleeveless qipao with gold floral pattern
C「米白」
young East Asian woman, early 20s, long wavy black hair, cream sleeveless qipao with subtle embroidery
D「红」
young East Asian woman, early 20s, short black hair, red silk embroidered qipao

4. 场景设定（锁定描述词）
dark wooden mahjong table with ivory tiles,
blue-and-white porcelain tea cups and teapot,
carved wooden lattice screens, warm glowing lantern

5. 分镜提示词（8 镜，中英对照）
用法：英文部分直接投喂视频模型；先用文生图跑出首帧，满意后再图生视频。
运镜原则：几乎全固定机位，只做"呼吸感"微动 —— 这是原片高级感的核心。
第 1 镜（0–4s）粉旗袍胸口特写
中文：粉旗袍女子上半身特写，珍珠盘扣，暖光在丝绸上流动，轻微呼吸起伏
English: Close-up of woman A torso in pink silk embroidered qipao, pearl brooch at collar. Very subtle motion: gentle breathing, warm lamplight flickering delicately across the silk. [风格母版]
第 2 镜（4–8s）摆牌手部特写
中文：纤细的手把一张象牙麻将牌竖起摆上桌，粉色旗袍在背景虚化处
English: Extreme close-up of delicate feminine hands placing an ivory mahjong tile upright on the dark wooden table, fingers moving with elegant precision, blurred pink silk qipao in background. [风格母版]
第 3 镜（8–11s）粉旗袍手臂中近景
中文：粉旗袍女子手臂优雅搭在桌沿，前景是麻将牌，几乎静止，只有光影微动
English: Medium close-up of woman A, arm resting gracefully on the mahjong table edge, ivory tiles in foreground. Near-still: only warm light shimmering softly on the silk. [风格母版]
第 4 镜（11–14s）粉衣女子垂眸浅笑特写（全片情绪最高点，"巧笑倩兮"）
中文：粉衣女子面部特写，眼帘低垂，嘴角含一丝浅笑，缓慢眨一次眼
English: Close-up of woman A face, eyes lowered with a soft gentle smile. Very subtle motion: she blinks slowly once, lips barely move, warm light playing softly on her face. [风格母版]
第 5 镜（14–19s）四人全景
中文：四位女子围桌打麻将全景，伸手摸牌、码牌，红衣女子举起青花茶杯
English: Wide shot of all four women (A pink, B black-gold, C cream, D red) playing mahjong around the table. Natural subtle movements: reaching for tiles, arranging walls, woman D lifting her blue-and-white porcelain tea cup. [风格母版 + 场景设定]
第 6 镜（19–23s）双人中景
中文：黑金旗袍与米白旗袍女子中景，前景双手推牌，眼神交汇、淡淡一笑
English: Medium shot of woman B and woman C at the table, hands in foreground gracefully pushing ivory tiles, soft glances and faint smiles exchanged. [风格母版]
第 7 镜（23–27s）门后朦胧远景（梦幻转场）
中文：隔着深色门框/格扇看牌桌，红衣与米白衣女子身影柔焦，尘埃在光柱里浮动
English: Dreamy shot through a dark doorway at the women playing mahjong, dark foreground softly out of focus, woman D and woman C visible through it, faint warm haze drifting, dust motes floating in light shafts. Slow drifting motion only. [风格母版]
第 8 镜（27–30s）三人欢笑收尾
中文：三位女子围桌轻轻欢笑，头部微动，手落在牌与茶杯旁，暖光收尾
English: Medium-wide shot of three women laughing softly together around the mahjong table, gentle natural laughter, slight head movements, hands resting near tiles and tea cups. [风格母版]

6. 通用禁令（Negative prompt，每个镜头都加）
text, subtitles, watermark, logo, modern clothing, modern furniture,
smartphone, extra fingers, deformed hands, distorted face,
oversaturated colors, cartoon, anime, harsh lighting

7. 音频方案
BGM（原片方案）：《相思遥》
原唱：魏玉慧（玉慧同学），另有郭雨昂、张含韵版本
切入点：从副歌 "初见她漫步溪桥下" 开始
一遍副歌约 28–30 秒，正好铺满 30 秒成片
注意：版权歌曲，需自行提供音源；不同版本副歌起始时间不同
  （玉慧同学版约 00:45–00:49），拿到音频后按波形定位切入点

8. 剪辑装配
每镜取中间最稳的 4.25 秒，8 镜 × 4.25s，镜间 0.57s 交叉淡化 → 正好 30 秒
统一 1280×720、24fps，加轻微暗角（vignette）统一观感
音频淡入 0.5s、26s 起淡出；人声/主音乐优先，垫乐压低约 12dB

要点：
① 全固定机位+呼吸级微动（拒绝大运镜） 
② 人物描述词逐字锁定 
③ 特写与全景 1:1 交替的节奏。
````
