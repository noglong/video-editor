## Prompt

````text
Muse Video Prompt
Format lock: Aspect ratio 3:4 (source 1080x1450, 30 fps; generate at 1080x1440, 30 fps) — the output MUST use exactly this ratio; no cropping, no reframing, no letterbox change.
Total runtime: about 59 seconds in 6 clips.

A first-person POV sci-fi comedy in photorealistic doll-like CG: a modern young man wakes in the year 2350, in one of humanity's last cities — a place run by beautiful android girls and giant mechs, where men are extinct. THE CAMERA IS THE MAN. He is never seen; only his voice is heard. Every girl speaks to the lens, points at the lens, studies the lens. The whole joke and the whole charm live in that direct address.
Identity — THE GUARD: A girl of about nineteen with long black hair, twin side locks and small black horn-like hair accessories; glossy doll features, sly bright eyes, pink-gloss lips. A silver chrome cropped tech jacket over a black outfit with a short skirt and a slim choker. Cheeky and menacing in turns, always performing for the lens.
Identity — THE LITTLE GUARD: A shorter, younger girl with pink-purple gradient hair in a matching chrome outfit; mostly soft-focus or mid-frame; she exists to be scolded.
Identity — THE ELDER SISTER: A tall woman with very long straight white-blue hair to her hips, pale porcelain skin, calm appraising eyes. A white top under a transparent clear-vinyl long coat, a black tech collar with metal fittings at her throat. She never hurries and never raises her voice; she evaluates the camera like a specimen she might keep.
Identity — THE TRIO: Three resident girls of the forest city — one silver-blonde bob, one brown-haired, one green-haired — in metallic silver dresses, curious as children at a zoo.
Identity — THE MAN (voice only): a young man's Mandarin voice, bewildered, increasingly panicked; he is the camera, never visible, no hands, no body.
World & scenes: (A) a dark mech hangar — a row of giant white bipedal mechs with glowing blue ring cores in their chests, a control console with holographic screens; (B) a neon corridor washed in pink and purple light strips; (C) a red-lit security doorway; (D) an indoor forest city — wet stone paths, wooden houses, drifting mist, floating holographic billboards, lush green canopy overhead.
Music and sound: a light, curious sci-fi score — soft synth pads and plucked notes, comedic timing, never horror; servo hums and hangar ambience under scene A; the girls' voices are clear Mandarin, spoken straight into the lens.
Camera: POV handheld feel throughout — the small sway of a person standing, breathing, following; girls repeatedly break the fourth wall by design; hard cuts between beats.

CLIP 1 of 6 — source 0:00-0:09.8. Scene A: the peeping and the discovery.
Sound (this clip): hangar hum, soft mech servo idle; the Guard's two lines, very close to the microphone of the scene.
THE RHYTHM & FORCE (this clip): stealth, then a face arriving too close — the comedy is the invasion of the lens. If she keeps a polite distance, it is wrong.
EMOTION ARC (this clip): she plays cat-and-mouse for one second, then owns him completely.
Dialogue — The Guard (~0:06-0:09, into the lens): "你是谁，为什么偷窥我"
Dialogue — The Guard (~0:09, pointing): "差点就被你发现了"
[0.0-5.5s] Shot 1 — POV wide, slow creeping drift, scene A: from behind a console, the row of giant white mechs with blue ring cores, holographic screens glowing; the camera peeks and settles like someone hiding. Avoid: any person in frame; fast movement; the mechs moving.
[5.5-9.8s] Shot 2 — face macro series, hard little push-ins, scene A: the Guard's face suddenly fills the screen — a sly smile straight into the lens, closer still until her eyes and nose are a macro landscape, then she pulls back a fraction and points directly at the camera as she delivers the two lines. Avoid: her looking anywhere but the lens; a frightened expression; subtitles appearing.
````
